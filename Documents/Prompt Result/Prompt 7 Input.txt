# 4. STEP 2 — REFERENCE RESEARCH ANALYSIS

The architecture references research including:

### Zhao et al., 2024

**Temperature field spatiotemporal modeling of lithium-ion battery pack configured sparse temperature sensors**

Journal of Energy Storage, Vol. 97, 113005.

Use this primarily for:

```text
Sparse sensing
Spatial temperature field reconstruction
Spatiotemporal modeling
Sensor placement
Pack-level temperature estimation
```

The paper proposes a spatial-temporal model for temperature-field reconstruction with sparse sensors and evaluates operating conditions, sensor count and sensor locations.

### Wang et al., 2024

**A Deep Transfer Operator Learning Method for Temperature Field Reconstruction in a Lithium-Ion Battery Pack**

IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, 20(6), 8089–8101.

Use this primarily for:

```text
Physics-informed temperature-field reconstruction
Transfer learning
Unseen operating conditions
Generalization
Space-time feature learning
```

The published work describes transformer-based spatiotemporal modeling, thermal PDE constraints and domain-adversarial transfer mechanisms for unseen scenarios.

### Zhou et al., 2024

**Distributed Thermal Monitoring for Large-Format Li-Ion Battery Under Limited Sensing**

IEEE Transactions on Transportation Electrification, 10(2), 3206–3217.

Use this primarily for:

```text
Distributed thermal monitoring
Limited sensing
2-D temperature reconstruction
Thermal fault monitoring
```

The documented approach reconstructs the temperature field using sparse measurements and performs thermal fault monitoring through a distributed thermal framework.

Determine which methodology is most relevant to Step 2 and why.
