## 2. Review Paper Eligibility
- **Explicit Call Inclusion:** The Call for Papers explicitly invites:
  > *"The conference proceeding invites academics and industry researchers for publication of papers explaining literature review, problem statement, methodologies with appropriate results. Acceptance will be based on significant and innovative content, originality and unpublished quality work."*
- **Review Scope:** Eligible as a critical or systematic review paper when paired with a methodological evaluation framework, comparative literature benchmarks, and an architectural synthesis addressing tangible research gaps.

## 4. Manuscript Formatting & Page Limits
- **Page Limit:** **6 printed pages maximum** (strictly observed for the base registration fee).
- **Excess Pages:** Up to 2 excess pages may be permitted subject to excess page fees (INR 1000/$25 per extra page), but staying within the 6-page limit ensures zero penalty and compliance with reviewer preferences.
- **Format:** IEEE standard two-column conference proceedings format, A4 paper size, 10pt Times New Roman, standard IEEE margins.
- **Accepted File Type:** PDF (initial submission in CMT).
- **Publication Venue:** Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion into **IEEE Xplore** digital library.

## 5. Strict Ethics, Plagiarism & AI Policies (Non-Negotiable)
- **Plagiarism Screening:** All manuscripts undergo automated similarity screening (Turnitin / IEEE CrossCheck).
  - **Overall Similarity Limit:** Must be strictly **below 20%** (inclusive of references).
  - **Single-Source Similarity Limit:** Must not exceed **10%** from any single source.
- **AI-Generated Content Ceiling:**
  > *"The proportion of AI-generated material in a submission must not exceed 10% of the total content."*
  - **Mandate:** AI must ONLY be utilized for source discovery, metadata structuring, evidence matrices, comparative synthesis tables, and linguistic polish.
  - Substantive intellectual arguments, critical domain analysis, and manuscript prose are owned and authored by the human researcher.
- **Authorship Freeze:** Once submitted and assigned a Paper ID in CMT, **no changes to authorship** (order, additions, or deletions) will be allowed.
