# 08 — IEEE ESCI-2027 Submission Checklist & Portal Guide

**Submission Portal:** Microsoft CMT 3: `https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/ESCI2027`  
**Target Deadline:** 15 October 2026  
**Conference:** 9th IEEE International Conference on Emerging Smart Computing and Informatics (ESCI-2027)

---

## Pre-Submission Verification Checklist

- [ ] **1. Exact Track Selection:** Select **TRACK 1: SMART COMPUTING** in CMT author console.
- [ ] **2. Author Order & Affiliations:**
  - Verify full author names, institutional affiliations, city, country, and valid professional/institutional email addresses.
  - *Critical Conference Policy Reminder:* Once submitted and assigned a Paper ID, **no changes to authorship** are permitted by ESCI-2027.
- [ ] **3. Paper Title & Abstract Concordance:**
  - Verify that the title and abstract pasted into CMT match the PDF manuscript character-for-character.
- [ ] **4. Page Limit & Formatting:**
  - Ensure total manuscript length is **strictly 6 pages** (standard IEEE two-column, A4 format).
  - Verify all text, figures, tables, and bibliography fit within 6 pages to avoid excess page charges.
- [ ] **5. Similarity & Plagiarism Screening:**
  - Verify that the overall similarity score on Turnitin / iThenticate is strictly **< 20%**.
  - Verify that similarity from any single published source is strictly **< 10%**.
- [ ] **6. Strict AI Policy Compliance:**
  - Confirm that AI-generated prose accounts for **less than 10%** of the manuscript text.
  - Ensure substantive domain synthesis, critique, and experimental analyses are original human authorship.
- [ ] **7. Bibliography & Reference Traceability:**
  - Check that all 32 in-text citations map sequentially to entries [1]–[32] in the references list.
  - Verify all DOIs are active and accurately linked.
- [ ] **8. PDF Compliance:**
  - Ensure all fonts are embedded and subsetted.
  - Ensure no active macros or security passwords are set on the submission PDF.

---

## CMT Online Submission Walkthrough

1. **Sign In:** Navigate to `https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/ESCI2027` and log in with your Microsoft CMT credentials.
2. **Create New Submission:** Click on the `Create new submission` tab and select `Track 1: Smart Computing`.
3. **Enter Metadata:**
   - **Title:** `A Critical Review of Deep Learning-Based Virtual Temperature Sensing for Lithium-Ion Batteries: CNN–LSTM Models, Trust-Aware Data Validation, and Data Provenance`
   - **Abstract:** Paste finalized abstract text.
   - **Subject Areas / Keywords:** `Deep Learning`, `Virtual Sensing`, `Battery Thermal Management`, `CNN-LSTM`, `Cybersecurity`, `Sensor Anomaly Detection`, `Data Provenance`, `Blockchain`.
4. **Author Details:** Add all co-authors by email and ensure correct primary contact author checkbox is selected.
5. **Upload Manuscript:** Upload the compiled 6-page PDF.
6. **Declarations & Consent:** Agree to IEEE publication ethics and plagiarism policy statements.
7. **Submit & Record Paper ID:** Click `Submit`. Record the assigned CMT Paper ID and save the submission confirmation email.
